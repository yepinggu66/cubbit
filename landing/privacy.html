Cubbit for Windows

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Cubbit is a small Windows desktop app. This policy explains what the app collects, what it never collects, and how to have your data deleted. It is short on purpose.

1. The short version

Cubbit needs very little to work. We do not collect your screen or your keystrokes. The gestures, notes and voice messages you send to a paired desktop are uploaded so they can be delivered, kept for a limited time, and then deleted. You can ask us to delete your data at any time by emailing support@cubbit.app.

2. What we collect

  • Pairing and license data. The pairing code and the minimum identifiers needed to connect two desktops and to check that a Couple Pack license is valid. You can start Cubbit without an account.
  • What you send. The gestures, notes and voice messages you choose to send to a paired desktop, and the time they were sent. Voice messages are limited to 60 seconds.
  • Support messages. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write in your message.

3. What we never collect

  • Your screen contents, screenshots or recordings of your desktop.
  • Your keystrokes or what you type in other apps.
  • Anything from your microphone other than the voice messages you choose to record and send.

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for advertising. This website is a static page: it sets no cookies, uses no analytics, and loads no third-party fonts or scripts.

4. Voice messages and couple data

When you send a gesture, note or voice message to a paired desktop, it is uploaded from your PC and delivered to the app on the other desktop, which is how the two cubes stay in sync. We do not sell these messages, and we do not use them for advertising. They are used only to deliver what you sent.

5. Where your data is stored

Pairing records and messages are stored on the servers that run Cubbit's sync service, while they are waiting to be delivered and for the retention period described below. This website is static and does not send anything to those servers. If you need the hosting region for a compliance review, ask us at support@cubbit.app.

6. How long we keep it

  • Pairing records: while the two desktops are paired, plus 30 days after you unpair.
  • Notes and voice messages: up to 30 days after delivery, then deleted.
  • Support emails: up to 12 months after the conversation ends.

We may delete data sooner, including when you ask us to.

7. Deleting your data

Email support@cubbit.app with the subject line "Delete my data" and the pairing code or email address you use with Cubbit. You do not need an account to make the request. We will delete the data we hold within 30 days, and we will confirm by email when it is done.

8. Your rights in the EU and UK

If you are in the European Union, the EEA or the UK, you have the right to access your personal data, correct it, delete it, restrict how it is used, receive a copy in a portable format, object to its use, and withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing. You can make any of these requests by email at support@cubbit.app. We answer within one month and do not charge for it. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will be described on this page before they take effect.

Data controller: Cubbit, an independent studio. Questions about this policy: support@cubbit.app.
