What it does

Four small things it does.

No dashboard. No to-do list. No feed to keep up with. Just a cube on your desktop, and a direct line to the person at the other one.

42 expressions

42 moods, no delay

Shy, sleepy, grumpy, and a blush when it gets poked. Forty-two expressions, each one an instant reaction to what you do.

7 gestures

Pokes cross the gap

Poke your cube and their cube reacts on their desktop, right away. Seven gestures to pick from: poke, heart, hug, kiss, high-five, hold hands, goodnight.

Notes + 60s voice

Say it without a call

Send a note or a 60-second voice message. It appears in a speech bubble above their cube, so they find it the next time they look at their desktop.

Days together

One check-in a day

Check in together each day and the cube keeps the count: how many days you have been together.

No second desktop? No problem.

It works solo too.

Pairing is optional. On its own, the cube still runs through all 42 expressions, still reacts when you poke and play with it, still takes skins, and still turns a good moment into a screenshot or a GIF.

FAQ

Three quick questions.

Do we both need to buy it?

No. Pairing works on the free version. The Couple Year Pass ($39.99) covers two people for a year; nothing renews automatically.

Do I need an account to try it?

No. Download it and start: there is a free trial and a free version, and you do not need an account or a card to begin.

What happens to my pokes and voice messages?

Cubbit does not capture your screen or log your keystrokes. The gestures, notes and voice messages you send are uploaded so they can reach the other desktop, kept for a limited time, then deleted. You can read the details in the privacy policy.
